Pricing questions, answered plainly

What does the setup fee cover?

Real work: provisioning your dedicated phone routing, connecting Brooke to your practice's schedule, configuring your escalation contacts, and testing the full call flow with your team before launch.

What counts as a call?

A call Brooke actually answers and handles. Hang-ups, wrong numbers, and robocalls don't count against your included volume.

What happens if we go over {{ site.company.included_calls }} calls?

Nothing scary. We'll reach out, show you the numbers, and add an overage block at a fair rate. High call volume is a good problem — we price it like one, not like a penalty.

Why no annual contract?

Because we've been the practice locked into one. If Brooke isn't earning her keep, you should be able to leave — that pressure keeps us honest and keeps the product improving.

Is there really no other cost?

The monthly fee and the one-time setup are it. Telephone infrastructure and AI processing are included — no per-minute meters, no "platform fees" appearing on invoice three.

Do you offer a trial?

The demo line is the trial — call {{ site.company.demo_number }} and put Brooke through her paces. And since there's no contract, your real trial is simply your first month.
